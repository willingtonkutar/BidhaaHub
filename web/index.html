Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Click "Sign In" to access the registration form. Fill in your details and create your account.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept card payments via Stripe and M-Pesa via IntaSend. Cash on Delivery is also available in select areas.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery typically takes 24-48 hours. Express delivery (where available) can be 4-6 hours.

Can I track my order?

Yes! Visit the "My Orders" page to view detailed tracking information for all your orders.
